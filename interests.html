Passion area
Healthcare & Data

I'm deeply interested in how data can transform healthcare — from improving diagnostic accuracy to reducing administrative burden to surfacing patterns that save lives. The intersection of health systems and data engineering feels like one of the most meaningful places I can apply my skills.

I follow research in clinical AI, health data interoperability (FHIR, HL7), and how large-scale data infrastructure can support better population health decisions. It's a space I'd love to work in one day.
