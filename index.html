About

Hi, I'm Malika.

I love anything data — the way it tells stories, surfaces patterns, and drives decisions that actually matter. I'm drawn to the intersection of data, technology, and business, and I get genuinely excited by problems that sit right at that crossroads.

I'm especially interested in healthcare and how data can be used to improve outcomes, reduce inefficiency, and ultimately help people. Whether it's building a lakehouse architecture for a Fortune 500 or deploying an ML model from scratch, I care deeply about making the technical work translate into real-world impact.

I love solving hard technical problems — the kind where the solution isn't obvious, where you have to dig in, rethink assumptions, and design something that actually holds up at scale. Currently at Ernst & Young in New York and pursuing my M.S. in CS at Georgia Tech, I'm always looking for the next challenge where data meets something that matters.
